TITLE: What is a Standard 2-Phase evaluation?
PRODUCT: CFD
CONTENT: The Standard 2-Phase evaluation is a two-stage assessment where traders must meet profit targets while respecting risk limits. Phase 1 requires an 8% profit target. Phase 2 requires a 5% profit target. There is no time limit on either phase. Minimum trading days: 5 per phase.
TAGS: ["2-phase", "evaluation", "profit target", "standard"]
===END===
TITLE: What are the Daily and Monthly Loss Limits?
PRODUCT: CFD
CONTENT: Daily Loss Limit (DLL) restricts how much you can lose in a single day. Monthly Loss Limit (MLL) restricts total losses over the evaluation or funded period. For Standard 2-Phase: DLL = 5%, MLL = 10%. For Standard 1-Phase: DLL = 3%, MLL = 6%. Both are calculated from the starting balance.
TAGS: ["loss limit", "DLL", "MLL", "risk", "drawdown"]
===END===
TITLE: How does the reward split work?
PRODUCT: CFD
CONTENT: On funded accounts, traders receive 80% of profits as their reward share. With the 95% Lifetime Reward add-on (purchased at checkout), this increases to 95%. Direct Start accounts begin at 70% for Tiers 1-2, reaching 80% at Tier 3. Payouts are processed within 24 hours of request.
TAGS: ["reward", "payout", "profit split", "80%", "95%"]
===END===
TITLE: What is the Growth Program?
PRODUCT: CFD
CONTENT: The Growth Program allows funded traders to increase their account size by 25% per stage. Requirements: at least 2 months of trading, 4 reward cycles, and minimum 4% growth each cycle. Maximum account size: $4,000,000. Only available for Standard 1-Phase and 2-Phase accounts (not Basic Lite or Direct Start).
TAGS: ["scale-up", "growth", "account size", "program"]
===END===
TITLE: Can I hold trades over the weekend?
PRODUCT: CFD
CONTENT: During the evaluation phase, weekend holding is allowed on CFD accounts. However, on funded (live) accounts, all positions must be closed before the weekend. Failure to close positions before the weekend on a funded account may result in a warning or rule violation.
TAGS: ["weekend", "holding", "funded", "rule"]
